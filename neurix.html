How It Works

Five steps from
fragmented data to recovered revenue.

01

Connect — 48-hour integration

Pre-built connectors to SAP, Salesforce, Oracle, NetSuite, HubSpot, Ironclad, Zuora, and your billing system. No data migration. No long implementation cycles. API-first, read-only access to your existing stack.

✓ No disruption to existing workflow
02

Map — build the revenue graph

Neurix constructs a directed graph of every revenue dollar: what was promised → what was delivered → what was billed → what was collected. Proprietary entity resolution links a contract clause to an invoice line item to a CRM note in real time.

✓ Cross-system entity linking
03

Detect — 23 categories of revenue anomaly

Unbilled services. Unauthorized discounts. Contract price non-compliance. Early payment terms not captured. Revenue recognized off-cycle. Each is flagged with source citations and a precise dollar impact. No manual investigation required.

✓ Ranked by dollar impact
04

Act — prescriptive, not just descriptive

Neurix doesn't surface a problem and leave you to solve it. It generates a ranked action queue with specific remediation steps, draft invoices, and contract citations — ready to send with one approval.

✓ One-click action queue
05

Learn — the model compounds on your data

Every action taken or dismissed trains Neurix's detection model on your specific business patterns. After 90 days, anomaly detection accuracy surpasses any general-purpose tool. After 12 months, the model knows your revenue architecture better than any analyst does.

The Founder

Built by someone who
lived the problem.

Background

Kush Jain spent six years at the intersection of enterprise finance and software — first as a finance analyst at a large manufacturing conglomerate, where he spent two years manually reconciling billing discrepancies across SAP and Salesforce, and later as a product lead at a B2B SaaS company where he saw the same problem from the other side of the table.

The insight

Every quarter, Kush's team would run a revenue reconciliation exercise that took three analysts two weeks to complete. The consistent finding: 4–6% of contracted revenue was never billed, disputed, or silently written off. The data to prevent it always existed. The system to connect it didn't.


"The signals were there weeks before the cash was gone. We just had no way to read them at scale. That's the entire thesis behind Neurix."

Why now

Large language models can now read and reason over B2B contracts at human-level accuracy — something that was computationally intractable three years ago.

The SAP S/4HANA migration wave has opened API access that enterprise teams previously had no programmatic way to use.

CFOs under post-2023 margin pressure are actively looking for internal cash recovery — not another software spend.

The mission

Neurix exists to give enterprise finance teams a real-time, complete view of their own revenue — and the intelligence to act on it before the money is gone. The company is built on the conviction that the best AI products don't replace the finance team; they make the finance team 10x more effective.
